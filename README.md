*About the Project

Millions of people fall victim to online scams every year, with financial fraud being a major concern.

According to the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), more than 32.8 lakh (3.28 million) cyber fraud complaints have been registered in India. Reports have also highlighted cases of people being harassed and blackmailed through fraudulent instant loan applications.

This is why we created Scan & Stop — an application designed to help users identify potentially fake loan applications, websites, and suspicious messages before they take any further action.

The application allows users to verify loan-related information and check whether a lending entity appears in the RBI's list of registered NBFCs. Based on the available information, it calculates a risk factor and provides a warning to help users make safer decisions.

Our goal is simple: STOP users before they become victims of fake instant loan scams.

The application is designed especially for people with little or no knowledge of cybersecurity, making it simple and accessible for users from different backgrounds.

---

* How It Works

1. Enter Information
   Enter loan-related information such as a website, app, or suspicious message.

2. Scan & Verify
   The application scans the provided information and checks the available details.

3. Check Lending Entity
   The lending entity is checked against relevant RBI/NBFC information.

4. Risk Analysis
   The available information is analyzed to determine the potential level of risk.

5. Get a Warning
   The application displays a risk factor and warning to help the user make a safer decision.

6. Emergency Assistance
   If a user has already fallen victim to a scam, the Emergency section provides guidance on the steps they can take and how to report the incident.

* Features

🚨 Emergency Section

If a user has already become a victim of a fake loan or online scam, the Emergency section provides step-by-step guidance on what to do and how to report the incident.

🌐 Bilingual Support

The application is available in English and Hindi, allowing users to choose the language they are most comfortable with.

❓ FAQs

Users can access frequently asked questions to quickly understand what they should do in common scam-related situations.

🌓 Light & Dark Theme

Users can switch between light and dark themes according to their preference.

👥 User-Friendly Interface

The application is designed to be simple and accessible, even for users with little or no knowledge of cybersecurity.

It is intended for users from different backgrounds, including farmers, local workers, employees, and others who may not be familiar with online financial scams.

🛑 Early Warning

The interface is designed to clearly highlight potential risks and warn users before they provide their personal or financial information to suspicious loan applications or websites.
